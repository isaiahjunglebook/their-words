# Tag Registry — Their Words

Every quote-level tag in the canon, with a definition and one example of the
kind of language that earns it. Tags are created, merged, split, and retired
only through owner-approved taxonomy changes — every change is logged in
`TAXONOMY-CHANGELOG.md`.

---

## the-lie

**Definition:** The man states, in his own words, the story he has been telling
himself that keeps him stuck — the internal justification he presents as fact.

**Example:** "I'm not an angry guy, I'm just tired. Anyone would snap in my
house."

## deferral

**Definition:** Postponing presence, connection, or change to a future
condition that never arrives. **Tag note — log the verb TENSE:** present tense
= a live, current need; future tense = a deferral ("I'll be more present when
work slows down").

**Example:** "I keep telling myself I'll be more present when work slows down.
It never slows down."

## father

**Definition:** Any description of the man's own father — what he did, didn't
do, said, modeled, or withheld — including vows to be different.

**Example:** "My dad was in the house but he was never in the room, if that
makes sense."

## map-language-adopted

**Definition:** The man spontaneously uses the program's language or frameworks
as his own — evidence the map is becoming his territory.

**Example:** "I caught myself mid-deferral on Tuesday and just… stopped."

## dream-outcome

**Definition:** The man's description of the future he actually wants — his
words for what winning looks like, at home, with his kids, in himself.

**Example:** "I want my son to hear the garage door and run to it, not away
from it."

## objection

**Definition:** Stated resistance to the program, the work, or the ask —
price, time, skepticism, fear — in the man's exact words. Raw material for
sales pages.

**Example:** "Honestly I almost didn't book this call. Talking about feelings
with other guys sounds like my nightmare."

## testimonial-seed

**Definition:** A statement of change or result, in the man's words, that
could grow into a testimonial with his permission.

**Example:** "My wife asked me last night what's gotten into me. That's never
happened in eleven years."

## founder-story-resonance

**Definition:** A man's response to the owner's story where it visibly lands —
recognition, "that's me," being seen. The quote captured is always the man's
words, never the owner's.

**Example:** "When you said the part about performing fatherhood instead of
living it — that's me. That's exactly me."
